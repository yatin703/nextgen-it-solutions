2. Standard Operating Procedure: Sales & Inquiries

This SOP governs how all prospective client inquiries, web form submissions, and tender Bill of Quantities (BOQ) uploads are recorded, evaluated, and transitioned into closed projects.

1 Inquiry Capture & Lead Ingestion

Inquiries arrive automatically via the web portal (/quote or /contact) or direct WhatsApp/Phone. Every submission automatically creates a unique record in the database with status New (e.g. LEAD-1005).

2 CRM Verification & BOQ Download

The sales desk opens the Mini-CRM dashboard at /admin/leads. The coordinator verifies client details (Company Name, Location, Phone) and downloads the attached BOQ file (PDF/Excel) from the "View BOQ" link.

3 First Response Protocol (SLA < 2 Hours)

The coordinator clicks the direct WhatsApp button on the lead row or calls the client. Confirm project scope, ask clarifying questions (number of CCTV points, ceiling height, building distances), and update status to Contacted.

4 Site Survey & Technical Costing

If the project requires physical verification, dispatch a field engineer within 24 hours. The engineering team applies authorized B2B vendor pricing (Cisco, Hikvision, Sophos, Dell) and formats an official PDF quotation.

5 Quotation Dispatch & Pipeline Tracking

Send quotation via email and WhatsApp. In the CRM, change lead status to Quotation Sent and record the quotation number in the internal notes field.

6 Deal Closing & Handover

Upon receiving Purchase Order (PO) or advance payment, update status to Won. If the client defers or cancels, update status to Lost with explanatory notes.

3. SOP: Technical Field Engineering Operations

This SOP provides compulsory technical standards for cabling, CCTV deployment, server configuration, and quality certification on client sites.

3.1 Physical Site Survey Checklist

Check Item Technical Standard Risk Mitigation
Cable Pathway Distances Copper CAT6 runs must NOT exceed 90 meters from switch to IO. If >90m, mandate fiber optic link or intermediary PoE switch.
Electromagnetic Interference Keep data cables >300mm away from 415V 3-phase industrial power cables. Use shielded CAT6A STP or armored fiber in machine rooms.
Electrical Earthing Server rack & switch earthing must measure <2.0 Ohms ground resistance. Prevents equipment damage from industrial surges or lightning.
CCTV Lens Selection Use 2.8mm for wide indoor rooms; 4mm/6mm for factory shop floor corridors; 12mm+ for entry gate number plates. Eliminates blind spots and provides crisp evidence.

3.2 Certified Installation & Rack Dressing Standards

  • Conduit Protection: All cables must run inside heavy-duty rigid PVC or galvanized iron (GI) conduits with metal saddles every 1 meter. No hanging or loose cables permitted.
  • Printed Ferrule Labeling: Every single cable must have machine-printed heat-shrink ferrule tags at both ends (e.g. RACK1-SW2-PORT14). Hand-written tape is strictly prohibited.
  • Rack Cable Management: Use 1U brush panels and horizontal cable managers. Bundle patch cords with reusable hook-and-loop velcro straps (never plastic zip ties).
  • Optical Fiber LIU: Fusion-spliced pigtails must be neatly coiled inside the Light Interface Unit (LIU) tray with minimum bend radius >30mm.
  • Testing & Certification: 100% of terminated network points must be tested using a calibrated Fluke Cable Analyzer. Export PDF wiremap & throughput certificates for the client handover binder.

3.3 CCTV Quality Assurance Protocol

  • Verify weatherproof IP67 cable gland seals on all outdoor dome and bullet cameras.
  • Set camera encoding to H.265+ smart compression to maximize recording retention (30–90 days).
  • Configure AI intrusion detection lines across boundary walls and calibrate sensitivity to filter out stray animals and rain.
  • Set up secure remote viewing credentials with strong alphanumeric passwords and role-based permissions (Security Guard view vs Director playback).

4.2 Monthly Preventive Maintenance Schedule

Check RAID array health, verify automated cloud & NAS backups, inspect server event logs, and verify UPS battery runtime.

Week 2: Network & Firewall Audit

Inspect managed switch port errors, check firewall threat logs, verify VPN connections, and clean network rack dust filters.

Week 3: CCTV & Surveillance Audit

Clean camera optical lenses, verify HDD health in NVR, confirm continuous recording on all channels, and adjust night IR illumination.

Week 4: Desktop & Workstation Health

Perform internal dust blowing, test thermal paste on CPUs, verify antivirus definitions, and clear temporary files.
